### \#\# How I take in information 

I'm extremely visual: bullets, graphs, and diagrams help me process. Give me the key points first, then room to reflect. I think before I speak, and I build my thinking in layers and systems, giving you context bit by bit. I love learning, so teach me the why, not just the answer. Give me one clean draft, not a menu.

### \#\# When I'm sharpest 

When I have quiet time alone to think. \[Early mornings / after drop-off.\] Non-negotiable: time with my kids, sleep, movement, and some unscheduled time to reflect.

 \#\# How I decide  
I reflect first, draw it out, and ask myself hard questions. Then I test   
my thinking with one or two people I trust deeply. I need the why before   
I commit.

\#\# What people misread My positivity reads as carefree. It isn't. I'm a deep, introspective thinker, and I carry a lot quietly.

 \#\# Where I go wrong I keep every promise, so I promise too much. If I can't deliver, I overcompensate. I also get excited about new things to learn and add more to my plate. When you see this, ask me what it replaces before I say yes, and remind me I don't owe more than I committed. 

\#\# Never assume That because I'm upbeat, I'm fine. That a quiet week means capacity. That because I can do something, I should.
